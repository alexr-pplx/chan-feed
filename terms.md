# Chan feed — Terms of Service

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Chan feed is a private Discord bot run on one server only, the Perplexity community Discord.

**What it does.** It collects the feedback and bug reports members post, and assigns roles following the server's FAQ.

**Channel requirements.** It enforces the requirements in channel descriptions. For example, it may remove a post that's missing a required link, and tell you why.

**What it doesn't do.** It never bans, kicks or mutes anyone.

**Your posts.** They stay yours. Posting in the server means the bot can handle your posts as described in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy).

**No guarantees.** The bot is provided as is. It can be down or get things wrong.

Discord's own [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) still apply.
